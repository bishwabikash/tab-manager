Tab Manager Pro

Organize tabs by domain with automatic grouping, search, duplicate detection, and split view support.

Tab Manager Pro is a powerful productivity tool designed to help you manage specific browsing habits. It automatically groups your tabs by domain, identifying and organizing them into color-coded groups for easier navigation.

Key Features:

Automatic Tab Grouping
Automatically organizes all your open tabs into groups based on their domain. This helps declutter your tab strip and makes it easier to find what you are looking for.

Duplicate Tab Detection
Identify and close duplicate tabs to save memory and reduce clutter. The extension scans all open tabs and provides a list of duplicates, allowing you to close them with a single click.

Search and Filter
Quickly find specific tabs using the built-in search bar. Filter tabs by title or URL to locate exactly what you need in seconds.

Recently Closed Tabs
Access and restore recently closed tabs directly from the extension. A dedicated history panel keeps track of your closed sessions.

Split View Support
Seamlessly manage tabs in split view. The extension detects and displays tabs that are currently in split view, ensuring you never lose track of your workspaces.

Memory Efficient
Designed with performance in mind. It uses efficient algorithms and caching mechanisms to ensure minimal impact on system resources.

Keyboard Shortcuts
- Ctrl+Shift+G: Group all tabs
- Ctrl+Shift+U: Ungroup all tabs

Privacy Focused
All processing happens locally on your device. No browsing data is sent to external servers.
