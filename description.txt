Too many tabs? Get them sorted in one click.

Tab Manager Pro groups your tabs by website — automatically, as you browse — so your tab strip finally makes sense again. Find any tab in a keystroke, wipe out duplicates instantly and bring back anything you closed by mistake.

✦ WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT

• Auto-grouping by site — All your GitHub tabs together, all your Stack Overflow answers together. Colour-coded, labelled and always tidy. Groups you've named yourself are never touched.

• Find any tab in a keystroke — Search titles and web addresses across the whole window, with every match highlighted.

• One-click duplicate cleanup — Spot tabs that are open twice and close the extras. Pinned tabs are always left alone. Prefer it hands-free? Let duplicates close automatically as you browse.

• Undo the "oops" — Restore recently closed tabs, or entire windows, from the History panel.

• Mute and sort from the popup — Silence a noisy tab without hunting for it, and sort any group A–Z.

• Dark or light — A polished theme either way, saved across sessions.

• Keyboard-friendly — Shortcuts for grouping and ungrouping, plus full keyboard navigation in the popup.

✦ YOU'RE IN CONTROL

Every feature is optional. Turn auto-grouping on or off, choose whether single-tab sites get their own group, collapse groups by default, and decide whether duplicates close automatically.

✦ PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Everything runs on your device. No accounts, no tracking, and no browsing data sent anywhere.

✦ KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS

• Ctrl+Shift+G (⌘+Shift+G on Mac) — group all tabs
• Ctrl+Shift+U (⌘+Shift+U on Mac) — ungroup all tabs
Change them anytime at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Works in Google Chrome and Brave.
