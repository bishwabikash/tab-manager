Welcome to Tab Manager Pro

Four quick steps and your tabs will sort themselves out. You can change any of this later in Settings.

Runs entirely on your device — nothing is sent anywhere
1

Pin it to your toolbar

Keep Tab Manager Pro one click away.

Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of your browser, then the pin next to Tab Manager Pro.
2

Choose how grouping works

Auto-grouping puts tabs from the same site into a colour-coded group as you browse. Groups you name yourself are never touched.

3

Open the popup

Click the Tab Manager Pro icon to see every tab in the window, sorted by site.

SearchFind any tab by title or address.
DuplicatesSpot tabs open twice and close the extras.
HistoryBring back recently closed tabs and windows.
Mute & sortSilence a noisy tab or sort a group A–Z.
4

Learn the shortcuts

Group or ungroup every tab in the window without opening anything.

    Good to know

    • Your own groups stay yours. Name a group (for example "Project X") and auto-grouping leaves it alone.
    • Links stay with their group. Open a link from a tab in a group and the new tab joins that group.
    • Avoid naming a group after a site ("GitHub", "Google") — the extension treats those names as its own site groups.
    • Pinned tabs are never grouped or closed, including by duplicate clean-up.
