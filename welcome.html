Tab Manager Pro

Automatically organise your tabs by website, find duplicates, and keep your browser clutter-free — all locally, no data ever leaves your device.

  • Auto-grouping — tabs from the same site are grouped with a colour label automatically
  • Duplicate detection — find and close duplicate tabs in one click, or automatically
  • Recently closed — restore any recently closed tab or window from the History panel
  • Mute & sort — mute noisy tabs and sort groups alphabetically right from the popup

All set! You can change settings any time via the extension options.

Keyboard shortcuts:  Ctrl+Shift+G to group  ·  Ctrl+Shift+U to ungroup
